Graphic design & 3D modelling

character design 3D modelling animation
software: GIMP Blender

My design book for the Graphic Design course: a steampunk female dwarf for a children's tale, developed from initial sketches to textured 3D models of the character and her room, and brought to life in a final animation of the character in her room.

the brief

A character for a children's tale: female, dwarf, steampunk. I designed her in 2D, then built her and a matching room in 3D.

the process

Sketch, ink, colour and model sheet in 2D. Then furniture and room blocked out in Blender, the character modelled, and materials and textures added to both.

the result

A complete character and room design from first sketch to textured 3D models, finished with an animation of the character in her room.
