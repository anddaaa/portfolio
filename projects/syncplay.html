SyncPlay

streaming platform app design
software: Canva OBS

A concept for a next-generation streaming platform that unifies video (films/series) and audio (music/podcasts) into one interactive social ecosystem. Built around AI-driven "Watch & Listen Parties" to solve fragmented social viewing for Gen Z audiences. Developed as a group project for the Streaming Platforms course.

the problem

OTT platforms offer passive consumption, forcing users to fragment their experience across video apps and Discord/WhatsApp chats.

the concept

A unified communication and player layer with millisecond synchronization, instant audio/video mode switching, and AI match-making.

the business

A Freemium model featuring Multi-DRM (Widevine/FairPlay) and forensic watermarking to protect studio copyrights during social streams.

app architecture & interface flow

Detailed design breakdown covering user onboarding, synchronization tech, social matchmaking, and dual audio/video UI.

Section 01 Onboarding & AI Recommendation Engine

Solves the cold-start problem through an interactive genre quiz during sign-up. The AI calculates an "Affinity Level" percentage badge on titles to suggest ideal group watch options based on overlapping friend tastes.

Section 02 Synchronized Rooms, Replays & Social Matching

Rooms use WebRTC for ultra-low latency voice/webcam feeds and WebSockets to sync playback controls instantly across viewers. Includes "Blind Watch Parties", "Streamer Mode" (recordable VOD reaction overlays), and a global "Switch to Music" mode.
